2026 Philippine Statutory Deduction Rates at a Glance

Summary of mandatory employee and employer schedules used across SuweldoTools calculators.

View Methodology & Legal Sources →

SSS (Social Security System)

15% of MSC

5.0% Employee / 10.0% Employer (+ ₱10 or ₱30 EC). Evaluated on Monthly Salary Credit from ₱5,000 to ₱35,000 (MPF/WISP above ₱20,000).

Basis: RA 11199 · Published SSS Contribution Schedule

View SSS Contribution Table →

PhilHealth (NHIP)

5.0% Premium

2.5% Employee / 2.5% Employer equal split. Applied to monthly basic salary with a ₱10,000 floor (₱250 EE) and ₱100,000 ceiling (₱2,500 EE).

Basis: RA 11223 (UHC Act) · PhilHealth Advisory

Open PhilHealth Calculator →

Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF)

₱200 Max EE Share

2.0% Employee / 2.0% Employer (1% EE if salary ≤ ₱1,500) evaluated up to the ₱10,000 maximum monthly fund salary ceiling.

Basis: HDMF Circular No. 460

Open Pag-IBIG & MP2 Calculator →

BIR Withholding Tax

0% up to ₱20,833/mo

Graduated 0% to 35% monthly tax table applied to taxable income (Gross pay minus mandatory SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG employee shares).

Basis: RA 10963 (TRAIN Law) Effective 2023+

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using SuweldoTools and Philippine salary computation.

What is SuweldoTools? ▼
SuweldoTools is a free, web-based suite of salary and payroll calculators designed specifically for Philippine wage earners, jobseekers, freelancers, and human resource professionals.
Are these salary calculators completely free to use? ▼
Yes. All salary calculation tools on SuweldoTools are 100% free with no sign-ups, subscriptions, or paywalls required.
Does SuweldoTools save or transmit my salary information? ▼
No. All computations run entirely on your device's browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not store, log, or transmit your financial inputs to any server, as detailed in our Privacy Policy.
Which Philippine government deductions are supported? ▼
Our tools compute mandatory employee deductions for the Social Security System (SSS), Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG), and Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) withholding tax.
Can these calculators replace an employer's official payslip? ▼
No. Our tools provide informational estimates based on standard statutory schedules, as outlined in our Disclaimer. Company-specific factors such as loan amortizations, voluntary Pag-IBIG MP2 contributions, health card dependents, unpaid absences, and year-end annualization can cause your actual payroll to differ. Always verify with your company HR or payroll specialist.
