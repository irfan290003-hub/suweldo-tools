Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview and Core Privacy Approach

SuweldoTools (accessible via suweldotools.com) is an independently operated informational website founded and maintained by Mohammad Irfan. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the website, how calculator inputs are handled in your browser, and what third-party services are involved when pages load.

Calculator Inputs Stay in Your Browser: SuweldoTools does not collect, transmit, record, or store the salary numbers, allowance figures, working hours, or payroll deduction values you enter into our calculators.

All salary computations (such as in our Take-Home Pay Calculator), Monthly Salary Credit (MSC) lookups, and TRAIN Law withholding tax estimates are executed locally within your device's browser using client-side JavaScript. No entered payroll figures are sent over the network or saved in a database. When you close or refresh your browser tab, your calculator inputs are cleared (except when temporarily held in sessionStorage during a direct language switch within the same tab, as described in Section 2).

While calculator computations run locally, public pages do make network requests to third-party services to load site-wide announcements (Supabase) and, only if you accept analytics cookies, to measure general website traffic (Google Analytics 4), as described below.

2. Cookies and Browser Storage

SuweldoTools does not set advertising cookies or cross-site ad-tracking cookies. Public pages use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure website traffic and general usage only after you explicitly accept analytics in the Cookie Preferences banner. Until you click Accept Cookies, GA4 is strictly blocked and no Google Analytics scripts or analytics cookies are loaded. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may set and read first-party analytics cookies (such as _ga and property-specific analytics cookies like _ga_E3Z69Z6Y0J) in your browser to distinguish visits and aggregate session statistics. If you click Reject or ignore the banner, analytics remains disabled.

In addition, SuweldoTools uses standard browser storage on your device for website functionality:

  • localStorage: Stores your analytics cookie consent choice (suweldotools_cookie_consent, including your accepted or rejected status, decision timestamp, six-month expiration timestamp, and consent version) for six (6) months so you are not asked on every page load; stores your selected language preference between English and Filipino/Tagalog (suweldotools_lang and suweldotools_explicit_lang); and stores a content hash when you dismiss or click through a site-wide announcement popup or banner (suweldotools_announcement_dismissed) so the same announcement is not repeatedly displayed on subsequent page loads.
  • sessionStorage: Temporarily holds your current calculator form values in your active browser tab when you switch between the English and Filipino/Tagalog versions of a calculator (suweldotools_calc_handoff, which is immediately removed via sessionStorage.removeItem once the destination page loads those values) and records if you dismiss the language-suggestion prompt during your current tab session (suweldotools_lang_banner_dismissed).

3. Site Announcements and Supabase Requests

Public pages include a client-side script (/src/announcement-popup.ts) that uses Supabase to check whether an active site-wide notice or announcement (such as an update regarding calculator tables or website notices) has been published.

  • When the request is made: On every public page load (all pages outside /admin), when Supabase project credentials are configured, your browser sends an automated HTTP GET request to the configured Supabase REST API endpoint (/rest/v1/site_announcements) querying for an active published announcement (slot=eq.published and is_active=eq.true).
  • Nature of the request: The request is anonymous with respect to individual visitors. It uses only the public Supabase publishable/anonymous key in the apikey and Authorization: Bearer request headers and does not authenticate individual users or send user session tokens.
  • Information transmitted: Our application code does not send any calculator inputs, cookies, or localStorage values in this request. However, because your browser connects directly to the Supabase endpoint over HTTPS, standard network request metadata—including your IP address, browser User-Agent, referring page or origin header, and request timestamp—is automatically transmitted to Supabase as part of normal HTTP communication.
  • Local dismissal state: When an active announcement is returned, your browser computes a deterministic hash of the announcement content and checks suweldotools_announcement_dismissed in your browser's localStorage. Whether you view or dismiss the announcement is handled locally in your browser and is not sent back to Supabase.

4. Analytics and Advertising

SuweldoTools uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) on public pages only when you accept analytics in the Cookie Preferences banner. When enabled, Google Analytics may collect standard usage information (such as pages visited and session duration), device and browser metadata (such as device category, operating system, and browser version), and general interaction data. In our website's application code, salary figures, allowances, working-hour inputs, and payroll deduction results entered into our calculators are processed locally in your browser and are not transmitted to Google Analytics through our code, URLs, or custom analytics events (note that SuweldoTools cannot control third-party browser extensions or external software installed on your device).

Your analytics consent choice (accepted or rejected) is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key suweldotools_cookie_consent for six (6) months, after which the expired entry is removed and the banner will prompt you again. You can reopen Cookie Settings from the website footer on any public page at any time to withdraw or change your choice. If you change your choice from accepted to rejected, SuweldoTools updates suweldotools_cookie_consent to rejected, prevents further GA4 initialization or tracking, and attempts to clear existing first-party GA4 cookies (_ga and _ga_*) across the website's applicable domain and paths.

SuweldoTools currently does not use Google AdSense or third-party advertising networks. In our site's GA4 tracking snippet, we configure allow_google_signals: false and allow_ad_personalization_signals: false to disable Google Signals and advertising personalization at the tag level (while noting that code-level tag settings are separate from Google Analytics property-level Admin console settings). If advertising services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Hosting, Infrastructure Logs, and Data Retention

When you access SuweldoTools or when your browser requests resources from third-party infrastructure providers (such as our web hosting and content delivery network provider, Supabase for announcement requests, and Google for analytics scripts), standard HTTP request logs (such as requesting IP address, browser user-agent, date and time of request, and requested URL) are automatically processed by those providers to deliver content, maintain network availability, and mitigate abuse.

SuweldoTools does not store personal calculator inputs on a server. Retention and processing of network logs, analytics records, and API requests are governed by the respective infrastructure and service providers' operational practices. If you contact us by email, your message and email address remain in our email inbox for the purpose of reviewing and responding to your inquiry.

6. Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

In recognition of general privacy and data minimization principles under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), SuweldoTools is designed so visitors can estimate salaries and statutory contributions in their browser without creating an account or submitting personal payroll records. This Privacy Policy is provided to transparently explain how the website operates and what external services are used; it does not represent a claim of formal legal certification, government accreditation, or third-party legal audit.

7. External References & Government Portals

Our guides and reference pages link to public government websites (listed on our Official Sources & References page), including the Social Security System (sss.gov.ph), Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (philhealth.gov.ph), Pag-IBIG Fund (pagibigfund.gov.ph), the Bureau of Internal Revenue (bir.gov.ph), and the Department of Labor and Employment (dole.gov.ph), as well as external social media platforms. We do not operate these external websites and encourage visitors to review their respective privacy policies.

8. Inquiries and Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how SuweldoTools handles information, you may contact the website operator at contact@suweldotools.com. For general inquiries, visit our Contact page, or review our Corrections Policy when reporting calculation discrepancies or content errors.

Privacy Reminder: Please do not include passwords, government ID numbers, bank account details, payslips, or other sensitive personal information in email submissions.
