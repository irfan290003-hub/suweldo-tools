Notice a Rate Revision or Edge Case?

Philippine statutory schedules (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR) can change when new government circulars or advisories are released. Public references used across the site are listed on the Official Sources & References page, and how calculator content is prepared is described in the Editorial Policy. Notices of updated circulars, content corrections, and calculation feedback from employees, HR personnel, accountants, and readers are welcome.

If you spot a calculation discrepancy, outdated reference, or new rate schedule, please email contact@suweldotools.com. To make the issue easier to check, please include:

  • Calculator name: The specific calculator or table where the discrepancy occurred.
  • Inputs used: The salary amount, pay frequency, or parameters entered (never include passwords, bank accounts, or sensitive personal data).
  • Calculation comparison: The result displayed versus the expected figure.
  • Official reference: A link to the official circular, advisory, or statutory schedule for rate adjustments.

For more details on how reported issues are reviewed and corrected when appropriate, please see the Corrections Policy.

Privacy Questions & Partnerships

Because SuweldoTools performs calculator computations client-side in your web browser, salary inputs entered into the calculators are not stored, logged, or transmitted to a server for calculation. If you have questions regarding data practices or client-side processing, please consult the Privacy Policy.

For advertising enquiries, educational collaborations, partnerships, or media questions, feel free to send an email to contact@suweldotools.com.

Please Note: SuweldoTools provides informational calculators and cannot provide individualized legal representation, personalized tax filing services, or mediation in employer-employee labor disputes.
