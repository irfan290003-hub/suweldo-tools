Notice a Rate Revision or Edge Case?

Philippine statutory tables (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR) are periodically updated through new circulars and advisories. While our team continuously monitors official government releases, we actively welcome notices and corrections from payroll practitioners, accountants, HR professionals, and employees.

If you notice an upcoming contribution tier adjustment, a newly published circular, or a specific formula edge case, please send an email to contact@suweldotools.com with a reference link to the official agency advisory. We review and update our annual rate tables promptly.
