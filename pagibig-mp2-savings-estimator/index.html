How Compounded MP2 Dividends Work

When you choose Compounded (End-of-Term Payout), your annual dividend is credited to your MP2 account balance at the end of each year. In the following year, both your cumulative principal deposits and all previously credited dividends earn the declared annual dividend rate. Regular monthly deposits earn pro-rated dividends based on the number of months they remain in the fund during the calendar year (an average weight of 6.5 / 12 months for uniform monthly deposits).

How Annual Dividend Payout Works

When you choose Annual Dividend Payout, Pag-IBIG disburses your earned dividend every year to your nominated bank account or Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card Plus. Because dividends are withdrawn yearly, only your cumulative principal contributions remain in the account to earn dividends in subsequent years, resulting in simple annual dividend growth rather than compounded growth.

Calculation Basis & Estimate Disclosure

  • Documented Calculation Assumptions: Prepared using publicly available government references (HDMF Circular No. 460; see the official sources and statutory references) — ₱1,500.00 low-salary threshold (1% employee / 2% employer), standard > ₱1,500.00 rate (2% employee / 2% employer), ₱10,000.00 Maximum Fund Salary (MFS) ceiling, and ₱200.00 standard maximum monthly employee share.
  • Requires Official Pag-IBIG Fund Confirmation: Future MP2 annual dividend rates (which are declared yearly by the Pag-IBIG Board of Trustees based on actual Fund net income and are never guaranteed in advance, as explained in the full disclaimer), exact month-by-month posting cutoff dates for mid-year MP2 account openings, and any current or future HDMF circular or MFS ceiling adjustment.

Frequently Asked Questions (Pag-IBIG & MP2)

1. How much is the mandatory Pag-IBIG contribution in 2026? ▼
Under HDMF Circular No. 460, employees earning over ₱1,500 per month contribute 2% of their monthly basic compensation, matched by a 2% employer counterpart, up to the ₱10,000 Maximum Fund Salary (MFS) ceiling. For anyone earning ₱10,000 or more per month, the mandatory employee deduction is ₱200.00/month (₱100.00 per semi-monthly kinsenas cutoff), plus ₱200.00/month from the employer. You can use our Take-Home Pay Calculator to see how mandatory Pag-IBIG I deductions combine with SSS, PhilHealth, and withholding tax when estimating your net take-home pay.
2. Are Pag-IBIG MP2 dividends tax-free? ▼
Yes. Under Republic Act No. 9679, all Pag-IBIG Regular Savings and Modified Pag-IBIG II (MP2) dividends are 100% exempt from the 20% final withholding tax normally charged on commercial bank time deposits and savings accounts.
3. Are MP2 dividend rates guaranteed? ▼
No. While your total principal contributions in MP2 are guaranteed by the Philippine government, annual dividend rates depend on Pag-IBIG Fund's actual net income each year. This estimator allows you to test different assumed dividend rates (such as 6%, 7%, or 8%) to plan safely.
