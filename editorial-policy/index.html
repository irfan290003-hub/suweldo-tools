Editorial Policy

How calculator information, public government references, and documented assumptions are prepared and maintained on SuweldoTools.

1. Publicly Available Government Sources

Calculator information, reference tables, and guides on SuweldoTools (suweldotools.com) are prepared using publicly available government sources and published contribution or tax schedules from the relevant Philippine government agencies—specifically the Social Security System (SSS), Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund), Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), and Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE).

The public government portals, circulars, and statutory schedules referenced across the website are listed on the Official Sources & References page.

2. Documented Assumptions and Calculator Rules

Each tool on the Calculators directory—including the Take-Home Pay Calculator—performs calculations using documented assumptions and the specific rules implemented in that calculator. Published brackets, salary floors and ceilings, contribution percentages, and withholding tax thresholds are translated into client-side browser code (see the Privacy Policy), with key assumptions and scope notes explained alongside each calculator.

3. Changes in Government Rules and Schedules

Philippine government rules, statutory contribution schedules, salary ceilings, and tax regulations can change when new laws, circulars, or agency advisories are issued. Because policies and schedules may change over time, calculator rules and reference content are updated when changes are identified and incorporated into the site.

4. Informational Estimates and Official Confirmation

All calculator outputs, breakdowns, and articles on SuweldoTools are provided strictly as general informational estimates, as outlined in the Disclaimer. Actual payroll figures may differ depending on employer-specific payroll policies, attendance adjustments, company benefits, loan deductions, or year-end tax annualization. Users should consult the relevant government agency (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR, or DOLE) or a qualified payroll, tax, or legal professional for official confirmation.

5. Independent Operation and Scope

SuweldoTools is an independently operated informational website founded and maintained by Mohammad Irfan. The website is not affiliated with or endorsed by any Philippine government agency, and does not claim formal audits, third-party certifications, continuous real-time rate monitoring, or professional accounting (CPA), payroll, tax, or legal credentials.

Reporting Errors or Outdated Information

Users can report suspected calculation errors, outdated contribution schedules, or unclear explanations through the Corrections Policy, the Contact page, or by emailing contact@suweldotools.com. Reported issues can be reviewed against publicly available official sources and corrections made when appropriate.
