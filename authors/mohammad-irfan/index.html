Mohammad Irfan

Founder & Website Developer

Website: SuweldoTools.com · Email: contact@suweldotools.com

Mohammad Irfan is the founder and website developer of SuweldoTools (suweldotools.com), an independent web project built to help Filipino workers understand their salary components, estimate net take-home pay, and explore mandatory government contribution calculations using free, client-side online tools.

About the Project

Understanding a Philippine payslip often involves checking multiple statutory tables—Monthly Salary Credits, premium sharing percentages, savings fund caps, and withholding tax brackets. Mohammad Irfan created SuweldoTools to present these published calculation rules in a clear, accessible, and fast web interface that works on both mobile phones and desktop browsers.

Every calculator on SuweldoTools runs directly in the user’s browser without requiring account registration or storing personal salary inputs on external servers. Pages are maintained in both English and Tagalog so workers, job applicants, and small teams across the Philippines can review estimates in the language they prefer.

Areas of Responsibility on SuweldoTools

As Founder and Website Developer, Mohammad Irfan is responsible for the technical development and day-to-day maintenance of SuweldoTools, including:

  • Web Application Architecture & Interface Design: Building responsive, mobile-friendly static HTML pages and interactive client-side calculators that load quickly and display itemized payroll breakdowns.
  • Calculator Implementation & Worked Examples: Translating published Philippine statutory contribution tables and withholding tax formulas into browser-side code and testing calculator outputs against sample salary scenarios.
  • Bilingual Content Structure: Maintaining parallel English and Tagalog calculator interfaces, explanatory guides, and reference pages.
  • Site Maintenance & Issue Resolution: Reviewing user feedback sent to contact@suweldotools.com and updating calculator tables or page text when discrepancies or rule updates are identified.

Official Sources, Editorial Standards & Corrections

SuweldoTools relies on publicly available schedules, circulars, and statutory guidelines published by Philippine government agencies—specifically the Social Security System (SSS), Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund), Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), and Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE).
