# SuweldoTools — Philippine Statutory Payroll Rules & Official Sources (2026)

This document establishes the authoritative reference data and official regulatory grounding for all salary, 13th month pay, and payroll deduction calculations implemented in SuweldoTools.

---

## 1. Social Security System (SSS)

### Governing Authority & Legal Basis
* **Agency:** Social Security System (SSS) — Republic of the Philippines
* **Primary Law:** Republic Act No. 11199 (The Social Security Act of 2018)
* **Implementing Circulars & Official Announcements:** 
  * **SSS Circular No. 2024-006** (Issued December 19, 2024: *Schedule of SSS Contributions for All Business Employers and Employees Effective January 2025*, which superseded and repealed the historical 2023–2024 schedule under SSS Circular No. 2022-033)
  * **Republic Act No. 11199 § 4(a)(1)** (Mandated statutory 15% rate schedule, ₱5,000 minimum MSC, and ₱35,000 maximum MSC effective January 2025)
  * **SSS Official Advisory:** "SSS Clarifies 1% Contribution Rate Hike; Bares Plans for 2025" (affirming 15% rate with ₱5,000 min MSC and ₱35,000 max MSC)
  * *Applicability to 2026:* Under RA 11199 § 4(a)(1), the 15% rate is the final scheduled biennial increase; the schedule in SSS Circular No. 2024-006 continues in force for 2026 unless amended by subsequent legislation or SSS issuance.
* **Official Portals:**
  * SSS Pay Contribution: [sss.gov.ph/pay-contribution/](https://www.sss.gov.ph/pay-contribution/)
  * SSS Circulars Directory: [sss.gov.ph/sss-circulars/](https://www.sss.gov.ph/sss-circulars/)
  * SSS EC Program: [sss.gov.ph/employees-compensation-program/](https://www.sss.gov.ph/employees-compensation-program/)
  * SSS Rate Clarification: [sss.gov.ph/news-and-updates/sss-clarifies-1-contribution-rate-hike-bares-plans-for-2025/](https://www.sss.gov.ph/news-and-updates/sss-clarifies-1-contribution-rate-hike-bares-plans-for-2025/)

### Statutory Rates & Parameters (2026)
* **Total Statutory Contribution Rate:** 15.0% of Monthly Salary Credit (MSC)
* **Employee Share:** 5.0% of MSC
* **Employer Share:** 10.0% of MSC
* **Minimum Monthly Salary Credit (MSC):** ₱5,000.00 (applies to salaries below ₱5,250.00)
  * *Minimum Employee Contribution:* ₱250.00 / month
  * *Minimum Employer Contribution:* ₱500.00 / month (plus ₱10.00 EC = ₱510.00)
* **Maximum Monthly Salary Credit (MSC):** ₱35,000.00 (applies to salaries ₱34,750.00 and above)
  * *Maximum Employee Contribution:* ₱1,750.00 / month (Regular ₱1,000.00 + WISP ₱750.00)
  * *Maximum Employer Contribution:* ₱3,500.00 / month (Regular ₱2,000.00 + WISP ₱1,500.00, plus ₱30.00 EC = ₱3,530.00)
* **MSC Bracket Step:** ₱500.00 per salary tier (61 total brackets from ₱5,000 to ₱35,000)

### Regular Social Security (SS) Program vs. Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF / MySSS Pension Booster)
* **Regular SS Program:** MSC is capped at ₱20,000.00.
  * Maximum Regular Employee Share: ₱20,000.00 × 5.0% = **₱1,000.00 / month**
  * Maximum Regular Employer Share: ₱20,000.00 × 10.0% = **₱2,000.00 / month**
* **Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF) / MySSS Pension Booster (formerly WISP):**
  * Mandated under RA 11199 for all private sector employees whose MSC exceeds ₱20,000.00 up to the ₱35,000.00 statutory ceiling.
  * In 2024, SSS officially rebranded the mandatory component of WISP as the **MySSS Pension Booster (Mandatory)**, also cited in tables as the **Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF)**.
  * The excess MSC above ₱20,000.00 (up to ₱15,000.00 excess) is allocated directly into this dedicated individual tax-free retirement/provident account.
  * Maximum MPF Employee Share: ₱15,000.00 × 5.0% = **₱750.00 / month**
  * Maximum MPF Employer Share: ₱15,000.00 × 10.0% = **₱1,500.00 / month**
* **Payslip Impact:** The employee's total deduction is always 5.0% of the aggregate MSC (up to ₱1,750.00).

### Employees' Compensation (EC) Program
* **Legal Basis:** Presidential Decree No. 626 (Employees' Compensation and State Insurance Fund) as amended; ECC Executive Order No. 33.
* **Liability:** The EC contribution is **100% employer-funded**. Employees are never deducted for EC.
* **Rate Schedule:**
  * For MSC below ₱15,000.00 (MSC ₱5,000 to ₱14,500): **₱10.00 / month** per employee
  * For MSC of ₱15,000.00 and above (MSC ₱15,000 to ₱35,000): **₱30.00 / month** per employee

## 2. Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth)

### Governing Authority & Legal Basis
* **Agency:** Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth)
* **Primary Law:** Republic Act No. 11223 (Universal Health Care Act) § 10
* **Official Portal:** [philhealth.gov.ph](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph)

### Statutory Rates & Parameters (2026)
* **Total Premium Rate:** 5.0% of monthly basic salary
* **Employee Share:** 2.5% of monthly basic salary (half of total premium)
* **Employer Share:** 2.5% of monthly basic salary (half of total premium)
* **Statutory Income Floor:** ₱10,000.00
  * For basic salaries at or below ₱10,000.00, contribution is computed based on ₱10,000.00.
  * *Minimum Employee Premium:* ₱250.00 / month
* **Statutory Income Ceiling:** ₱100,000.00
  * For basic salaries at or above ₱100,000.00, contribution is capped at the ₱100,000.00 ceiling.
  * *Maximum Employee Premium:* ₱2,500.00 / month

## 3. Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund / HDMF)

### Governing Authority & Legal Basis
* **Agency:** Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund / HDMF)
* **Primary Law:** Republic Act No. 9679 (Home Development Mutual Fund Law of 2009)
* **Implementing Regulation:** HDMF Circular No. 460 ("Guidelines on the Implementation of the Increase in the Maximum Monthly Fund Salary", effective February 2024 onwards)
* **Official Portal:** [pagibigfund.gov.ph](https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph)

### Statutory Rates & Parameters (2026)
* **Employee Contribution Rate:**
  * For monthly compensation of ₱1,500.00 or less: 1.0%
  * For monthly compensation over ₱1,500.00: 2.0%
* **Employer Contribution Rate:** 2.0%
* **Maximum Fund Salary (MFS):** Capped at ₱10,000.00 (increased from ₱5,000.00 under Circular 460)
  * *Maximum Standard Employee Contribution:* ₱10,000.00 × 2% = **₱200.00 / month**
  * *Maximum Employer Counterpart:* ₱10,000.00 × 2% = **₱200.00 / month**

## 4. Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) — Withholding Tax

### Governing Authority & Legal Basis
* **Agency:** Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
* **Primary Law:** Republic Act No. 10963 (Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion / TRAIN Law)
* **Exemption Statute:** Republic Act No. 9504 (Statutory Minimum Wage Earner Exemption)
* **Regulatory Tables:** BIR Revenue Memorandum Order & Revised Withholding Tax Tables (effective January 1, 2023 onwards)
* **Official Portal:** [bir.gov.ph](https://www.bir.gov.ph)

### Annual Tax Brackets (TRAIN Law Schedule)
1. **Tier 1 (Up to ₱250,000.00 annual taxable income):**
   * Tax: **₱0.00 (0%)**
2. **Tier 2 (Over ₱250,000.00 to ₱400,000.00):**
   * Tax: **15% of the excess over ₱250,000.00**
3. **Tier 3 (Over ₱400,000.00 to ₱800,000.00):**
   * Tax: **₱22,500.00 + 20% of the excess over ₱400,000.00**
4. **Tier 4 (Over ₱800,000.00 to ₱2,000,000.00):**
   * Tax: **₱102,500.00 + 25% of the excess over ₱800,000.00**
5. **Tier 5 (Over ₱2,000,000.00 to ₱8,000,000.00):**
   * Tax: **₱402,500.00 + 30% of the excess over ₱2,000,000.00**
6. **Tier 6 (Over ₱8,000,000.00):**
   * Tax: **₱2,202,500.00 + 35% of the excess over ₱8,000,000.00**

### Minimum Wage Earner Exemption
* Under RA 9504 and Section 24(A)(2) of the National Internal Revenue Code as amended by RA 10963:
  * Minimum wage earners are **100% exempt from income withholding tax** on their minimum wage, overtime pay, holiday pay, night shift differential, and hazard pay.
  * Mandatory SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG contributions still apply by law.

## 5. Computation Sequencing & Mathematical Formulas (Monthly Payroll)

1. **Gross Monthly Pay:**
   $$\text{Gross} = \text{Monthly Basic Salary} + \text{Taxable Monthly Allowances}$$
2. **Mandatory Employee Deductions:**
   $$\text{Total Mandatory} = \text{SSS} + \text{PhilHealth} + \text{Pag-IBIG}$$
3. **Monthly Taxable Compensation:**
   $$\text{Taxable Monthly} = \max(0, \text{Gross} - \text{Total Mandatory})$$
4. **Annualized Withholding Tax:**
   $$\text{Annual Taxable} = \text{Taxable Monthly} \times 12$$
   $$\text{Monthly Tax} = \frac{\text{Annual Tax from TRAIN Table}}{12}$$
   *(If Minimum Wage Earner checked: Monthly Tax = ₱0.00)*
5. **Total Payroll Deductions:**
   $$\text{Total Deductions} = \text{Total Mandatory} + \text{Monthly Tax}$$
6. **Net Take-Home Pay (Monthly Sahod):**
   $$\text{Net Pay} = \text{Gross} - \text{Total Deductions}$$
7. **Semi-Monthly Cutoff (Kinsenas):**
   $$\text{Cutoff Pay} = \frac{\text{Net Pay}}{2}$$

## 6. Philippine 13th Month Pay Rules & Statutory Guidance

### Governing Authority & Legal Basis
* **Agency:** Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) & Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)
* **Primary Law:** Presidential Decree No. 851 (Requiring all employers to pay their employees a 13th month pay, enacted December 16, 1975)
* **Implementing Rules:** Revised Guidelines on the Implementation of the 13th Month Pay Law (issued by DOLE on November 16, 1987)
* **Annual Directives:** DOLE Annual Labor Advisories on Payment of 13th Month Pay (e.g. Labor Advisory No. 25, Series of 2023)
* **Tax Code Statute:** Section 32(B)(7)(e) of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended by Republic Act No. 10963 (TRAIN Law)
* **Official Portals:**
  * DOLE: [dole.gov.ph](https://www.dole.gov.ph)
  * BIR: [bir.gov.ph](https://www.bir.gov.ph)

### Eligibility & Coverage
* **Coverage:** All rank-and-file employees in the private sector who have rendered at least one (1) month of service within the calendar year, regardless of their position, designation, employment status, or the method by which their wages are paid.
* **Payment Deadline:** Employers are mandated by law to pay the 13th month pay on or before **December 24** of each calendar year.
* **Separated / Resigned Employees:** An employee who resigned or whose services were terminated at any time before the payment of the 13th month pay is entitled to a prorated 13th month pay in proportion to the length of time worked within the calendar year.

### Calculation Formula
$$\text{Gross 13th Month Pay} = \frac{\text{Total Basic Salary Earned During the Calendar Year}}{12}$$

* For an employee with a fixed basic salary who worked a complete 12 months with no unpaid absences, this equals one month's basic salary.
* For an employee with salary adjustments during the year, unpaid absences (leave without pay / undertime), or who worked fewer than 12 months (e.g. hired mid-year or resigned early), the DOLE-mandated formula uses the **actual total basic salary earned**, not the latest monthly rate multiplied by 12.

### What Counts as "Basic Salary"
* **Generally Included:** All remunerations or earnings paid by an employer to an employee for regular services rendered.
* **Generally Excluded:** Unless integrated into the basic salary by individual employment contract, company policy, or Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA):
  * Overtime pay
  * Premium pay for work on rest days and special/regular holidays
  * Night shift differential
  * Unused vacation and sick leave cash conversion
  * Cost-of-living allowances (COLA), transportation, food, housing, and other fringe benefits
  * Profit-sharing bonuses and discretionary awards
  * Non-productivity commissions

### Statutory Tax Treatment (The ₱90,000 Benefit Cap)
* Under Section 32(B)(7)(e) of the Tax Code as amended by RA 10963 (TRAIN Law), 13th month pay and "other benefits" (such as productivity incentives, Christmas bonus, loyalty awards, and other bonuses of similar nature) are exempt from income tax up to a maximum aggregate threshold of **₱90,000.00** per calendar year.
* **Aggregation Rule:** The ₱90,000 ceiling applies to the combined sum of the 13th month pay PLUS all other qualifying benefits received within the tax year.
* **Taxable Excess:** Any cumulative amount exceeding ₱90,000 is included in the employee's gross taxable compensation income and subjected to standard graduated withholding tax during year-end tax annualization (BIR Form 2316). It is not a separate fixed tax, but rather an addition to taxable income.

## 7. Assumptions, Caveats & Disclaimer

* All calculations represent statutory private-sector employment standards.
* Company-specific variances (e.g. collective bargaining agreements, voluntary Pag-IBIG MP2 savings, loan amortizations, health card dependents, unpaid tardiness, and year-end tax reconciliation) can cause actual payroll and tax numbers to differ.
* The calculators on SuweldoTools provide informational estimates and do not constitute formal legal, financial, or tax advice. Users are encouraged to verify specifics with their employer's HR/payroll department or relevant government agency.
